About G & M

A family business with a reason behind it

G & M Pressure Wash and Property Services was started in 2004 by a family who wanted to give their son with special needs a real work experience.

That beginning still shapes how the team works today: trained crews, well-kept equipment and a commitment to doing every job properly for residential and commercial clients across Calgary.
